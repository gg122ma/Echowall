## WHAT IS ECHOWALL?

**One line**
> EchoWall is a browser-based campus knowledge platform for Malaysian matriculation students that
> anchors student knowledge to the campus places, communities and subjects it belongs to.

**Three sentences**
> EchoWall lets a student select a building on an interactive KMK campus map, read practical
> information about it — its purpose, its real weekly opening hours, whether it is open right now,
> its rules — and read or publish notes attached to that exact building. It also provides three
> layered student communities (all matriculation students, one college, one programme) where posts
> are Discussions or Questions, and where Questions can receive comments and replies and be marked
> solved. Alongside these, Echo Library organises academic material by programme, semester and
> subject code so a student can reach a past-year paper and its linked answer scheme directly, and
> Ask Echo answers campus questions from a curated local knowledge base.

**50-word functional summary**
> EchoWall is a trilingual campus knowledge platform for matriculation students. Students post
> notes onto specific campus buildings from an interactive map, browse practical building
> information and opening hours, ask and resolve questions across three community scopes, find
> past-year papers and answer schemes by subject code, and get guided campus answers from a local
> knowledge base.

**100-word functional summary**
> EchoWall is a browser-based, trilingual campus knowledge platform for Malaysian matriculation
> students. Its defining action: a student picks a building on the interactive KMK map and publishes
> a note there — the note becomes a map marker and an entry on that building's wall. Each building
> page shows purpose, structured weekly opening hours with a live open/closed status, and special
> rules. Three community scopes — all matriculation students, one college, one programme — carry
> Discussion and Question posts with comments, replies and a solved state. Echo Library organises
> real past-year papers and answer schemes by programme, semester and subject code. Ask Echo answers
> campus questions from a curated local knowledge base.

**200-word detailed product summary**
> EchoWall is a static, browser-based campus knowledge platform for Malaysian matriculation
> students, built as a working prototype in HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript with no server or
> database. Its core mechanism is place anchoring: on an interactive Leaflet map of Kolej
> Matrikulasi Kedah, a student selects one of 19 interactive building footprints and publishes a
> note there. The note appears both as a map marker and as an entry on that building's wall,
> because both read the same store. Thirty-two building profiles carry trilingual descriptions;
> nineteen carry structured weekly opening hours with a live open/closed status derived from the
> college's own facility information, plus purpose and special rules where recorded.
>
> Three community scopes — All KM Students, twelve colleges, and thirty-four programme walls — use
> the same sticky-note interface, with ten note shapes, ten colours and four categories, and
> anonymous or named publishing. Every post is a Discussion or a Question; Questions accept
> comments and one level of replies and can be marked solved.
>
> Echo Library organises academic material by programme, semester and subject code, exposing 2,284
> browsable records of which 377 include the real file, with 230 reciprocally linked question and
> answer-scheme pairs. Ask Echo answers campus questions from a curated, source-attributed local
> knowledge base of 41 records, with explicit refusal boundaries. The entire interface and all
> campus content are available in English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese, in light, dark or system
> themes.

## WHO IS IT FOR?

Malaysian matriculation-college students. KMK (Kolej Matrikulasi Kedah) is the fully built
reference campus; eleven further colleges are configured as an expandable framework. Secondary
audiences: college staff who would moderate contributed content.

## WHAT CORE PROBLEM DOES IT ADDRESS?

Campus knowledge is fragmented and non-durable. Practical place knowledge, peer answers and
academic material all live in chat groups, forwarded files and seniors' memories — none of which
have an address, and all of which reset with each intake. EchoWall gives each kind of knowledge a
destination: place knowledge belongs to a building, questions belong to an audience scope, and
academic files belong to a subject code.

*(This is a supported problem/solution relationship. It is **not** evidence that measured
improvement has occurred.)*

## THE SIX CORE CAPABILITY GROUPS

1. Place-anchored campus knowledge (map + buildings + building walls)
2. Layered student communities with a real Q&A workflow
3. Structured academic resource access (Echo Library)
4. Guided campus guidance (Ask Echo)
5. Multilingual, multi-theme, low-barrier access
6. Content quality and platform governance

---

## TOP 5 FUNCTIONS

1. **Echo Map → building → post a note there → it appears on that building's wall.**
2. **Building Detail** — purpose, weekly opening-hours table, open/closed now, special rules,
   photo gallery, entry to the building wall.
3. **Communities** — All KM Students / College / Jurusan, with Discussion and Question posts,
   comments, replies, solved state, filters, sort and search.
4. **Echo Library** — programme → semester → subject code → resource, with search, filters, real
   PDFs, and linked question ↔ answer-scheme pairs.
5. **Ask Echo** — local knowledge-base answers with an action that opens the matching building page
   or the map.

## TOP 5 SELLING POINTS

1. A student's note lives at the place it is about — post it on the map, and it is on that
   building's wall.
2. Real academic resources reachable through the student's own syllabus structure, and the file
   actually opens.
3. A question can be asked at the right scale, answered, and marked solved — so the answer survives.
4. Practical building information — purpose, real hours, open/closed now, rules — on the same
   screen as the place.
5. The whole product, including its campus content, works in English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese.

## TOP 3 DEFINING SELLING POINTS

1. **Campus knowledge has a place.**
2. **Student questions get resolved, not just posted.**
3. **Academic material is organised by the syllabus, and the file opens.**

## BEST SUPPORT PER JUDGING CRITERION

**IDEA (20%)** — place-anchored posting (map note = wall entry) · three deliberate audience scopes ·
the sticky-note metaphor with a real workflow underneath · organising the library by syllabus rather
than by source · anonymity as a first-class publishing choice.

**PRAKTIKAL & KEBOLEHGUNAAN (20%)** — Echo Library search → real PDF → linked answer scheme ·
building hours and open/closed status · map posting producing a visible result in two places ·
question → comment → solved completing on screen · three languages on the same page.
*Cost effectiveness is explicitly part of this criterion and is genuinely strong:* a static site
with no framework, no build step, no server, no database and no paid service.

**IMPAK & SUMBANGAN (25%)** — Echo Library reach across every student · All KM Students as a
cross-college scope · building walls as knowledge continuity between intakes · trilingual +
anonymous access as inclusion · the governance model as adoption readiness.
**Always write *supports / enables / is designed to*. Never *has improved* or *has helped N
students*.**

**VIDEO (15%)** — map → post → wall · question → comment → solved · library search → PDF → scheme ·
Ask Echo answer with its action · a three-language switch on one page.

**DOKUMENTASI (15%)** — the admin/moderation model, the study-manifest pipeline, the 13 test suites,
the storage architecture, and the honest limitations register.

## WHAT IS NEW SINCE THE OLD KMK VERSION

The old competition video corresponds to the ~1 August 2026 build. Its release artefact contains no
Echo Library, no Community V2, no comments, no admin subsystem — this is provable from the file list.

**Top 5 evolutions:**
1. **Echo Library exists** — 2,284 browsable records, 377 real files, 230 question↔scheme pairs.
   *No counterpart existed before.*
2. **All KM Students** — a cross-college community scope with 67 trilingual seeded posts.
3. **Comments, replies, Discussion/Question and Solved** — posts became conversations with a
   lifecycle.
4. **Building Detail became a real information page** — purpose, structured weekly hours, live
   open/closed status, special rules.
5. **Admin V2** — 5 roles, 8 permissions, unified moderation queue, audit trail, flag-only
   auto-assist; plus 13 test suites and ≈799 passing assertions where there were only seed
   validators.

**What carried over unchanged (do not present as new):** the sticky-note model (10 shapes,
10 colours, 4 categories), anonymous/named publishing, the Leaflet map and map posting, the Building
Wall, Ask Echo, three languages, three themes, registration, and the 696-note demo seed.

- ✗ **"1,017 notes" / "53 photo notes" / "Latest memory Aug 25, 2026."** Hardcoded display
  constants in `app-router.js`. Not computed from anything. **Zero seeded notes have a photo.**

  "83" while its wall holds 1 real note; Langkasuka reads "17" while holding 42.
- ✗ **"67 students posted."** Seed content, transcribed verbatim from a document, all anonymous.
- ✗ **"782 student posts."** 763 seeds + 19 built-in defaults.
- ✗ **"The vote counts show real engagement."** Generated values in a 0–87 range; seed votes are
  frozen and cannot change.
- ✗ **"Used across 12 matriculation colleges" / "12 campus maps."** 11 of 12 registries are empty
  and are labelled "Framework Preview" in the product itself.
- ✗ **"Fourteen focus buildings."** Stale UI copy — the code has 13 sidebar entries and 19
  interactive footprints.
- ✗ **"2,284 downloadable files."** 2,284 *metadata records*; **377** real files.
- ✗ **"Resources are verified / lecturer-approved."** All 2,468 records are `unverified`.
- ✗ **"Notes can be translated."** The translation endpoint is empty; the button always fails.
- ✗ **"Students can report posts."** No report button exists anywhere.
- ✗ **"Students can see building events."** No building has event data; both sections are always
  empty.
- ✗ **"Students can review or rate buildings."** Never implemented.
- ✗ **"AI-powered / generative AI / BISHENG-powered assistant."** Ask Echo is local retrieval over
  a 41-record knowledge base. No remote model is configured.
- ✗ **"AI moderates content automatically."** Rules only *flag*; a human decides.
- ✗ **"Real-time / cloud sync / multi-device / secure institutional accounts."** Browser-local only.
- ✗ **"Deployed / live / hosted / in production."** The GitHub Pages workflow publishes a stale
  ~1 August ZIP and is configured for a `master` branch while the repo is on `main`. **The current
  feature set has never been published.**
- ✗ **"WCAG compliant / fully accessible."** Affordances exist; no audit does.
- ✗ **Any figure for users, adoption, time saved, grades, satisfaction or reach.** None exists.

## MAIN PROTOTYPE LIMITATIONS

1. **Browser-local only.** All accounts, posts, comments, votes, preferences, roles and moderation
   state live in `localStorage` in one browser; uploads live in IndexedDB in that same browser.
   Nothing is shared between users or devices.
2. **No backend, database, API or realtime layer.** Front-end permission checks are not a security
   boundary — the code says so about itself.
3. **The current build has never been published.** Run it locally over HTTP
   (`python -m http.server 8000`); do not open it via `file://` (auth uses Web Crypto).
4. **The map needs an internet connection** for OpenStreetMap tiles; Google Fonts likewise. All
   building data is local.
5. **Community content is seeded**, not real activity. Only 12 of 34 Jurusan walls and 8 of 32
   building walls start with content; all 12 College General walls are empty and unlinked.
6. **Seed posts cannot be voted on and cannot be marked Solved by a normal user.** Demonstrate
   voting and Solved only on a post the demo account created.
7. **Building notes have no comments** — that is a deliberate product boundary, not a bug.
8. **No edit or delete of your own post**, and no user-facing report action.
9. **Only KMK has building data.** The other 11 colleges show a labelled "Framework Preview".
10. **Nothing in Echo Library is verified**, and Computer Science has no resources at all.
11. **No user study, adoption data, lecturer validation or measured outcome exists** anywhere in the
    project. Every impact statement must be framed as potential.
12. **No formal accessibility audit, mobile-device test or screen-reader test** has been recorded.

## THE THREE THINGS MOST LIKELY TO GO WRONG IN A SUBMISSION

1. **Quoting the homepage numbers.** They are fabricated. If a judge opens the site and asks where
   1,017 comes from, there is no answer. Use the verified numbers table above instead.
2. **Describing Ask Echo as AI.** It is local retrieval over 41 curated records with explicit
   refusal boundaries. That is a *better* story than a generic AI claim, and it is true.
3. **Saying "12 colleges" without "framework".** The switcher shows 12; only one has data. Every
   mention needs "designed to support" or "expandable framework" in the same sentence.

## THE ONE THING MOST LIKELY TO RAISE THE SCORE

**Impak dan Sumbangan is the heaviest criterion at 25%, and the rules explicitly ask for
*bukti bergambar/surat* — photographic evidence or letters. The project currently has none.**
No additional feature will improve that criterion as much as one real session with real students, a
lecturer's written comment, or photographs of the prototype being used. Everything the product can
honestly say about impact today is potential, not outcome.
